World Tour Runners – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026

This privacy policy explains how World Tour Runners (the "Game"), a mobile game developed by Muhammed Emin Korkunç, handles your personal data. In short: the Game does not collect any personal data, does not send any data over the internet, and does not share any data with third parties.

1. Data we collect

The Game does not collect any personal data such as your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or advertising ID. There are no accounts, logins or registration.

2. Game data stored on your device

So that you can continue where you left off, the following information is stored only on your own device and never leaves it:

  • Best score and collected in-game coins (no real-money value)
  • In-game outfits you have bought and are wearing (bought with in-game coins; there are no real-money purchases)
  • Stories you have watched and unlocked
  • Settings: music and sound volume, graphics quality, frame rate, vibration, camera shake, swipe sensitivity, intro movie preference

You can delete this data at any time by uninstalling the Game or clearing its data in your device settings.

3. Internet, advertising and analytics

The Game works offline. It contains no advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting service, no social media integration and no real-money in-app purchases. The analytics, advertising and crash-reporting services of the Unity engine used to build the Game are disabled.

4. Permissions

  • Vibration: used to vibrate the phone briefly on crashes and stumbles. It can be turned off in the Settings screen.
  • Internet: added by default to Android apps by the Unity engine. The Game does not use it to send or receive any data.

The Game does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage or photo permissions.

5. Children's privacy

The Game is suitable for players of all ages. Because no personal data is collected, no data from children under 13 (or the applicable age in your country) is collected either.

6. Third-party content

Some 3D models, images, fonts and music in the Game come from openly licensed or public-domain sources. This content is bundled inside the Game; the Game does not connect to these sources or share any data with them. The authors are credited in the Game's Credits screen.

7. Your rights

Your rights under applicable data protection laws, including the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK, No. 6698) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), are reserved. However, since the Game does not process any personal data about you, there are no records to access, correct or delete.

8. Changes

If this policy is updated, the new version will be published on this page with its effective date. If a feature that collects data (for example advertising or an online leaderboard) is ever added, this policy will be updated beforehand.
